Matching publisher config and tokenizer vocabulary are pinned by the

Its task is BERT token classification with 51 BIO label IDs.

Original model card lists beki/privy, gretelai/synthetic_pii_finance_multilingual,
and eriktks/conll2003 as training datasets. Dataset license terms should be
reviewed separately before external distribution; a published model-card
Apache-2.0 tag does not settle all downstream dataset rights. This model's
English-oriented training does not demonstrate Hindi/Hinglish coverage.
Model-reported accuracy is not a CAPTAIN screenshot accuracy claim.

This independent optional classifier only adds opaque OCR-word pixel masks or
returns full blackout. It cannot produce planner text, selectors, action refs,
raw model output or unredacted screenshot egress, and cannot undo existing
OCR/DOM/face/UI blackouts. Unsupported non-ASCII OCR and ambiguous model
output force full blackout. Model-integrity/loading failure is not evidence
that a screenshot is safe. TrueSight original bridge remains ready:false.

Phase 07 remains PARTIAL until genuine in-extension end-to-end tests,
privacy-recall and performance evidence establish release readiness.
